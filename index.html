Tripwith

A group trip has three problems: what goes on the plan, how to settle disagreements, and who owes whom. Tripwith does all three, with the whole group in it.

CollectEveryone drops in what they want to do. Places two or more people want float to the top.
DecideWhen two ideas compete for the same afternoon, it becomes a vote. Settle it, and the plan updates for everyone.
SplitLog what you paid and who it was for. Multi-currency, uneven splits, and the fewest transfers to settle up.
