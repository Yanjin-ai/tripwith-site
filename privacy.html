Privacy Policy

Tripwith (同行) · Effective 2 October 2026

Tripwith is a group trip planner. This page explains what the app stores, where, and why. It is written to be read, not skimmed; it is short because we collect little.

What we store

DataWhyWhere
Trip content you create — places, times, votes, comments, tasks, expenses, budgetsIt is the productOn your device. If you sign in and upload a trip, also on our server so your companions can see it
Your account: email address or Apple ID identifier, display nameSo you can invite people and sync across devices. Signing in is optionalOur server
Push notification tokenTo tell you when someone votes, settles a plan, assigns you a task, or a trip starts tomorrowOur server, only if you allow notifications
Device languageTo send notifications in your languageOur server, with the token

What we do not do

  • Location. The app asks for location only to show where you are on the trip map. Your location is never sent to our server or stored.
  • Tracking. No advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs from ad networks, no selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • Contacts, photos, calendar. Not accessed.

Who can see a trip

Only the members of that trip. Access control is enforced on the server for every row of data; a person who is not a member of a trip cannot read or change it, even with a modified app.

Where the server is

Hosted by Supabase in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1). Supabase processes data on our behalf under its privacy policy. Map search and directions are provided by Apple Maps on your device and are subject to Apple's privacy policy; exchange rates come from Frankfurter, which receives only the currency codes.

Your rights

  • Export. Any trip can be exported as a JSON file from the app (My trips → long-press → Export).
  • Delete your account. Account → Delete account. Your account and sign-in are removed from the server immediately and cannot be recovered. Trips you took part in stay with your companions; your name, votes and expenses in them remain but are no longer linked to any account. Trips on your device are kept.
  • Delete a trip. My trips → swipe to delete. If the trip was uploaded, it is removed from the server for everyone.
  • Leave a trip. Ask any member to remove you from Trip and travellers.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, these are your GDPR rights of access, portability and erasure; you may also contact us to exercise them.

Children

Tripwith is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app will show a notice.

隐私政策

同行 Tripwith · 2026 年 10 月 2 日生效

同行是一个多人旅行规划工具。这一页说明 App 存什么、存在哪、为什么。它很短，因为我们收集得很少。

我们存什么

数据为什么存在哪
你创建的行程内容——地点、时间、投票、评论、分工、账目、预算这就是产品本身你的设备上。如果你登录并上传了行程，也会存在我们的服务器上，这样同伴才能看到
账号：邮箱或 Apple ID 标识、显示名用于邀请同伴和多设备同步。登录是可选的服务器
推送令牌有人投票、敲定、分给你一件事、行程明天出发时通知你服务器，仅当你允许通知
设备语言用你的语言发通知服务器，与令牌一起

我们不做什么

  • 位置。App 请求位置只为了在行程地图上显示你在哪。你的位置不会发到服务器，也不会被存储。
  • 追踪。没有广告标识符，没有广告网络的分析 SDK，不向任何人出售或共享数据。
  • 通讯录、照片、日历。不访问。

谁能看到一趟行程

只有这趟行程的成员。权限在服务器上逐行强制执行；不是成员的人读不到也改不了，即使用改过的 App 也不行。

服务器在哪

由 Supabase 托管在新加坡（AWS ap-southeast-1）。地图搜索和路线由你设备上的 Apple 地图提供；汇率来自 Frankfurter，它只收到货币代码。

你的权利

  • 导出。任何行程都可以从 App 导出为 JSON 文件（我的行程 → 长按 → 导出）。
  • 删除账号。账号 → 删除账号。账号和登录方式立即从服务器删除，不能恢复。你参与过的行程留给同伴，其中你的名字、投票和账目保留但不再关联任何账号。设备上的行程保留。
  • 删除行程。我的行程 → 左滑删除。已上传的行程会从服务器上对所有人删除。
  • 退出行程。请任一成员在「行程与成员设置」里移除你。

儿童

同行不面向 13 岁以下（或你所在地数字同意年龄以下）的儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的数据。

变更

政策变更时，顶部的日期会更新，App 内会提示。
